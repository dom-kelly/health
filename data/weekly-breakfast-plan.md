# Breakfasts — The Week

**Breakfast 8am. Milk, whey and apple 3pm.**

Three variants across seven days. One 850ml Sistema bowl does all of them. **5g creatine every single day, no exceptions** — in the yoghurt on lift days, in a glass of water on the others.

| Day | 8am | Protein | 3pm | Protein |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** *(lift)* | Yoghurt bowl | 68g | Apple | — |
| **Tue** | Eggs + smoked salmon | 49g | Milk + whey, apple | 32g |
| **Wed** *(lift)* | Yoghurt bowl | 68g | Apple | — |
| **Thu** | Eggs + mackerel | 45g | Milk + whey, apple | 32g |
| **Fri** *(lift)* | Yoghurt bowl | 68g | Apple | — |
| **Sat** | Eggs + smoked salmon | 49g | Milk + whey, apple | 32g |
| **Sun** | Eggs + mackerel | 45g | Milk + whey, apple | 32g |

Weekly average **56g at breakfast alone** — still the whole daily RNI before lunch has happened.

On rest days the 3pm slot carries another 32g.

---

## Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 8am — Yoghurt bowl

**68g protein · ~715 kcal**

- 250g fat free Greek style yoghurt
- 30g whey concentrate
- 5g creatine monohydrate
- 40g porridge oats
- 100g frozen berry mix
- 20g seed mix
- 1 Brazil nut
- 250ml semi-skimmed milk, plain

**Method**

Build it the night before. Yoghurt into the bowl first, then the whey and creatine, and beat them in properly with a fork before anything else goes in — add them after the oats and you get clumps that never break up.

Oats on top, then the seeds, then the Brazil nut. Clip the lid on with the **vent closed** and put it in the fridge.

Add the berries frozen in the morning if you want the bowl to stay white; added the night before they thaw and bleed pink through the yoghurt. Tastes identical either way.

Eat cold, straight from the bowl. This one never sees the microwave. Drink the milk plain alongside — the bowl already has a scoop of whey in it, and 60g of powder in one morning is more powder than food.

> The berry mix is tart. If blackcurrants against fat free Greek yoghurt is too austere at 7am, a splash of the milk stirred through softens it for almost nothing.

> One Brazil nut, and only on these three mornings. That's roughly a full day's selenium from a single nut, and the 30g serving on the pack would put you at or over the safe upper limit.

## Tuesday and Saturday, 8am — Eggs and smoked salmon

**49g protein · ~455 kcal**

- 3 large eggs
- 100g smoked salmon trimmings
- 100g frozen chopped spinach
- 1 slice seeded rye
- Black pepper, lemon
- 5g creatine in a glass of water

**Method**

Spinach into the bowl with a splash of water, lid on with the **vent open**, 90 seconds from frozen. Tip it into a sieve and press hard with the back of a spoon. Getting it properly dry is the difference between scrambled eggs and soup — this is the step people skip.

Wipe the bowl out. Crack the eggs in, beat with a pinch of salt, lid on, vent open. **30-second bursts, stirring between each** — about 90 seconds total. Stop while they still look glossy and slightly wet; they finish cooking in the residual heat of the bowl.

Salmon and spinach folded through **off the heat**. Pepper, lemon, rye on the side.

> Trimmings rather than slices: you're folding it into eggs, so the presentation is irrelevant and it's about half the price.

> This is a salty breakfast — around 3g from the salmon alone, half your daily limit. It's why it's twice a week and not four times.

## Thursday and Sunday, 8am — Eggs and mackerel

**45g protein · ~455 kcal**

- 3 large eggs
- 1 tin mackerel in spring water — **the whole tin**, not half
- 100g frozen chopped spinach
- 1 slice seeded rye
- Black pepper, lemon
- 5g creatine in a glass of water

**Method**

Identical to Tuesday. Spinach, squeeze, wipe, eggs in 30-second bursts.

Drain the tin and fold the mackerel through **off the heat**, breaking it into large flakes rather than stirring it to a paste. It's already fully cooked — a second cook is what makes tinned fish dry and makes it taste aggressively of itself.

A teaspoon of Dijon beaten into the raw eggs works well against the oily fish, for no meaningful calories.

> Spring water, not olive oil. Roughly 20g protein at 193 kcal against 17g at 245, for the same money — you pour a third of the oil-packed tin down the sink.

## Every day, 3pm

**Lift days (Mon, Wed, Fri)** — 1 apple. The milk is already drunk plain with breakfast.

**Rest days (Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun)** — 250ml semi-skimmed milk with 30g whey, plus 1 apple. **32g protein · ~340 kcal**

Skin on the apple — that's where about half the fibre is.

---

## Notes

**The milk is doing two jobs.** 300mg of calcium and about 90mcg of iodine daily — iodine being the one nutrient the rest of the plan genuinely misses, since UK diets get most of theirs from milk. Buy conventional, not organic; organic milk runs around 40% lower in iodine.

**Rest days out-protein lift days** by 9–13g, which is the opposite of what you'd assume. That's fine — protein needs are flat across the week, and Tuesday is repairing Monday's session.

**Why the milk moved.** Muscle protein synthesis saturates somewhere near 40g a meal, so 81g at breakfast wasn't building anything 49g wouldn't. Taken at 3pm with the apple instead, it does three jobs at once: spreads the protein, bridges to a family dinner you don't control so you arrive less hungry, and still delivers the calcium and iodine.

**Eggs:** 3 × 4 rest mornings = 12 a week. One box.
