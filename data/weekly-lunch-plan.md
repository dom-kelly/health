# Lunches — The Week

**Lunch 1pm.** Two dishes, two cooking sessions, five weekday lunches. Huel at weekends when the family is around and lunch isn't your own decision.

| Day | Lunch | Protein | Fibre | kcal | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Tue** | Beef and bean chilli | 59g | 19g | 685 | Reheat — 2 min |

Weekly average **49g protein, 17g fibre, £2.54 a day.** Thirty-two minutes of cooking across the week.

**Chilli first, tuna second.** Chilli genuinely improves overnight, and running the tuna batch on Wednesday means Friday's portion is only two days old rather than four.

## Monday — Beef and Bean Chilli, 2 portions

**59g protein · 19g fibre · ~685 kcal each · 20 minutes**

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 350g 5% beef mince
- 2 tsp garlic paste
- 4 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp cocoa powder
- 2 tins chopped tomatoes (800g)
- 2 tins kidney beans (800g) — **undrained**
- 200g frozen chopped spinach
- 2 tsp red wine vinegar
- Black pepper

**Method**

Oil into a large pan over medium-high. Mince in, broken up, 8 minutes until browned with no pink. Double the mince needs a wider pan than you'd think — crowd it and it steams instead of browning.

Heat down. Garlic, cumin, paprika, chilli, oregano and cocoa in, stir 1 minute. Cooking the spices in the fat is what stops chilli tasting dusty.

Tomatoes and kidney beans in, **liquid and all**. Simmer 8 minutes.

Frozen spinach in, 3 minutes. Off the heat, vinegar and pepper. Taste — it wants more salt and more chilli than you think.

Eat one portion. Spread the other in a shallow container, cool fast, fridge within the hour.

> The cocoa adds no chocolate flavour. It deepens everything and makes 20 minutes taste like two hours.

> Beef mince is the best zinc and iron source in your week — the two nutrients otherwise running lowest. Having it twice is what closes that gap.

> Tuesday's portion will taste better than Monday's.

## Wednesday — Butter Bean Base, 3 portions

**49g protein · 18g fibre · ~555 kcal each · 12 minutes**

**For the base:**
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tsp garlic paste
- 3 tsp smoked paprika
- 1½ tsp chilli flakes
- 3 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tins chopped tomatoes (1200g)
- 3 tins butter beans (1200g) — **undrained**
- 3 bay leaves
- 300g frozen chopped spinach
- 3 tsp red wine vinegar

**Added fresh each day:**
- 1 tin tuna chunks in spring water, drained
- Black pepper

**Method**

Oil into a large pan over medium. Garlic, paprika, chilli and oregano, stir 30 seconds until fragrant — don't let the garlic colour or the paprika catch, it turns bitter fast.

Tomatoes and butter beans in, **liquid and all**, with the bay leaves. Simmer 6 minutes. The starch in the bean liquid thickens the sauce as it goes, which is the difference between a stew and things sitting in tomato juice.

Frozen spinach in, 3 minutes. Off the heat, remove the bay leaves, stir in the vinegar.

Divide into three. Eat one now. Cool the other two in shallow containers, fridge within the hour. **No tuna in the stored portions.**

**Each day:** reheat a portion until piping hot, take it off the heat, fold a drained tin of tuna through in large flakes, 30 seconds. Pepper, eat. Two minutes.

> This split exists for one reason: the base reheats perfectly because it's tins, and the tuna never gets reheated at all. Reheated tuna goes dry and tastes aggressively of itself.

> Large flakes, not stirred hard — stir it and it turns to paste.

> Friday's portion is two days old, inside the three-day limit. If you'd rather not think about it, freeze that one Wednesday and move it to the fridge Thursday night.

## Saturday and Sunday — Huel and a pear

**40g protein · 13g fibre · ~495 kcal**

- 90g Huel Black Edition in 400ml water
- 1 pear, skin on

Shake. Eat pear.

> The pear isn't garnish. Huel gives 8g of fibre against the stew's 18, and the pear's 5.5g brings the weekend close to level. Skin on — that's where half of it is.

> "Nutritionally complete" means complete per 2,000 kcal. A 400 kcal serving is about 20% of each micronutrient, not a full day's. Fine as two days in seven; it's why it isn't four.

---

## Notes

**The vinegar isn't optional in either dish.** Beans and tomatoes together are flat without acid. It's also cheaper and more reliably in the cupboard than lemons.

**Never drain the beans.** Both recipes. The tin liquid is what makes the sauce creamy.

**Optional upgrades, no extra time:** chopped sun-dried tomatoes in with the tinned ones, or a tablespoon of grated pecorino stirred into the bowl at the end — thickens the sauce and adds calcium.

**Where this leaves the day.** Combined with breakfast and the 3pm shake, you're at 117–159g of protein before dinner. Monday needs only 16g from dinner; the weekend needs 54–58g, entirely because of Huel. If a weekend dinner is going to be light, that's the day to put real meat or fish on the plate.
